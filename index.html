Research

My research aims to make specialized hardware easier to design and program. During my Ph.D. I built compilers, generators, and exploration tools for programmable accelerators; my current work asks how agents can carry out this kind of engineering reliably.

01

Agentic engineering for chip design

2026 – present

Coding agents make implementation cheap, but deciding when the work is correct and complete is still hard. I build workflows that keep the two apart: a builder agent works from a plan approved by a human, a reviewer from another vendor decides completion, and deterministic hooks enforce mechanical gates in between. I apply these workflows to architecture and EDA work; one public example is a 567-file migration of the gem5 build system, now in upstream review.

Creator of Humanize 1

Humanize / Humanfia

I created Humanize 1 at UCLA: an implementation and independent-review loop for coding agents. I am a founding member of Humanfia, where we extend this approach to longer engineering tasks.

02

Programmable accelerators and design automation

Ph.D. research, 2018 – 2026

At UCLA I worked on making specialized hardware general enough to reuse and practical to build: accelerator architectures for irregular and graph workloads, compilers and generators for programmable accelerators and FPGA overlays, networks-on-chip for multi-chiplet FPGA systems, and Loom, a full-stack framework for compiling to and exploring heterogeneous accelerator systems.

Ph.D. dissertation, 2026

Loom

A compiler and exploration framework for heterogeneous multi-core spatial accelerators, connecting a shared hardware model, compilation, simulation, and reproducible evaluation.

Research, 2025 - 2026

OverNoC / BenchNoC

Characterization of hardened FPGA networks-on-chip and hierarchical network overlays for multi-chiplet reconfigurable systems.

ISCA 2020 / MICRO 2022

DSAGEN / OverGen

Automated generation of programmable spatial architectures and FPGA overlays, bringing compilation, hardware implementation, and design-space exploration into one flow.
